The learner first sees one representation change, then its actual role and sync consumers. The old-role branch is an explicit uncoordinated risk. The coordinated approved design is followed through identity, role and attempted target results before the lesson expands to reused logic, manager/lifecycle variants, rollout scope and recovery.
