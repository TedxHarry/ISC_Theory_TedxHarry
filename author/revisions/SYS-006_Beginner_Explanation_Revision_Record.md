## Explanation choices and whole-lesson author review

The opening locates the actual denied action and missing right before depending on a systems map. Separate identity/account records, source collection and account association supply the context for following department.

The mapping explanation places two collected values beside the selected identity value. Both inputs are available; the configured selection explains why OPS reaches identity department despite HR FIN being present. The authoritative-population distinction is explained after that concrete selection rather than as an opening glossary.

Role qualification is evaluated as false AND true before attempting to explain delivery. The role assignment records an access requirement; profile contents identify the viewing right, and later provisioning and native checks establish delivery and use. This avoids treating no expected new grant as a rejected write.

The successful continuation supplies processing applicability, supported and authorized delivery, actual native permission and controlled action evidence. Sign-in/session variations and four changed-evidence branches are separate conditions rather than invented explanations for the supplied main result.

The author read the entire saved lesson from a simulated beginner position, including middle/end, worked decisions and the warehouse transfer. Corrections removed an early session reference, replaced provisioning terminology before its introduction with the actual attempted-update distinction, introduced the technical actor before the write, and clarified no-new-grant wording in the diagram.

Edit/export denial is explicit at the opening and successful checks. The transfer explicitly supplies no prior role assignment or alternative grant, making its new-grant reasoning unambiguous while leaving downstream success for verification.

One editable diagram, two tables and a separate three-line criteria block were chosen for the data selection, decision and evidence work. They are not fixed lesson quotas. ## Example conditions and assessment reasoning

FIN means Finance, OPS means Operations and ACTIVE describes active employment only. The transfer effective time is 08:00, collected HR observation 08:10 and collected directory observation 08:15, in one hypothetical day/zone. These are not execution guarantees or processing intervals.

The main existing identity and accounts belong to the intended person. The one suitable enabled reporting account is the account used for sign-in and action checks. It initially lacks the sufficient viewing right and any alternative viewing grant; edit/export are unavailable. Applicable identity processing has evaluated the supplied inputs/configuration. Employment ACTIVE is not inferred to mean native enabled status or universal processing inclusion.

The HR-origin identity population uses directory department and HR employment status under the actual current mappings. The approved design instead requires HR department. The complete enabled automatic Finance Reports rule reads identity department FIN AND employment status ACTIVE; initially OPS/ACTIVE does not match. The role was not previously assigned, so this trace concerns no new assignment/grant through this path, not removal of every possible historical origin.

The standard role includes the Finance report viewing profile containing the source viewing entitlement. The chosen ordinary permission is usable after delivery without separate activation/future start and is sufficient under the application's current-permission rule. Role/profile configuration, services, write support, enabled provisioning, connectivity and authority are supplied for the successful continuation. Corrected eligible processing yields FIN/ACTIVE, assignment and accepted native addition, followed by current account and controlled viewing/denial evidence. Later collection is a supplied separate observation.

Directory OPS can remain after identity correction because the mapping is a read/selection instruction, not an automatic directory write. Attribute synchronization requires its own applicable configuration, mapped inputs, existing correlated accounts and supported target operation.

The four changed-evidence demonstrations keep old collected HR, absent role despite correct identity, rejected delivery and denial despite confirmed permission distinct. Wrong-session account and earlier permission information remain hypotheses unless established. Current technical actor privilege is different from recipient viewing permission.

The four worked decisions retain their distinct causal purposes. They do not use another collection to repair selection, change proper criteria to compensate for failed delivery, or broaden Finance access merely to make one employee qualify.

The warehouse transfer uses hypothetical WH2/WH1 location codes. It supplies a collected approved-register WH2, selected directory WH1, identity WH1, enabled WH2 role, no prior assignment/alternative grant, correct account association, absent right and completed applicable evaluation. Its answer checks shared impact before correction and requires later assignment/delivery/native-use evidence rather than assuming supported successful writes.
