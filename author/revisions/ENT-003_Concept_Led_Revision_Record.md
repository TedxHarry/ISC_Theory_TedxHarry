## Teaching decisions

Start with the equal-total misconception, establish object versus relationship, then count the actual account-group pairs and distinct groups/owners. Explain how collection and schema interpretation supply those records before tracing changed evidence.

Remove the fictional company/application brand, named employee, employee number and arbitrary account/group IDs. Equipment Production supplies necessary source context. First employee ordinary/admin and second employee account preserve account and identity distinctions without fictitious technical identifiers. Native asset-readers/asset-approvers/exception-reviewers/export-operators are meaningful example references; unmatched-group identifies a separate missing-detail case, not a real object.

Keep complete bounded coverage explicit: exactly three accounts and four groups, correct initial ownership/correlation and matching supported schemas, direct standing memberships, no additional access paths. The baseline is checked during an unchanged interval, not assumed to be an atomic snapshot. No other membership/object changes occur in the supplied later window.

Trace a complete observation sequence: verified baseline; approved native administrative-account removal; definition-only read with stale account evidence; fresh account values and reconciled counts. This avoids implying that successful collection performed a removal or every connector reads memberships the same way.

Two tables compare actual holder records and different measurements. Two diagrams show relationships and independent freshness. Eight original assessment purposes and the license transfer remain. Counts are descriptive, not a layout quota.

Full aggregation implementation, inherited-access design and catalog-retirement mechanisms are not invented here. Necessary supporting meanings are explained in place. Source context, account selection, read scope, current native evidence and effective permission remain distinct.
