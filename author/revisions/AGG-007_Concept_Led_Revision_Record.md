## Teaching decisions

The learner follows one disabled-but-existing application account through an unintended scope exclusion. External record, ISC account representation and identity are separated before deletion controls are introduced. The normal source setting and volume guard are explained before small-population rounding and different collection modes.

Neutral source/key/person-reference values remove the biography without losing identification or the distinct HR-authoritative relationship. Authoritative loss is an independent variation, allowing profile reassignment, nightly attempts and hidden identities to be explained without suggesting those events occurred in the main filter incident.

All seven documented deletion blockers remain, with meanings for the responsibilities involved. Hidden behavior is described precisely: no profile and no longer mapped values/listing, attached accounts treated as uncorrelated, not external access removal or erased history. The offboarding/recovery sections preserve operational judgment and independent target evidence. Structure follows the topic without word/visual quotas.
