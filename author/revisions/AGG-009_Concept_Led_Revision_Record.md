## Teaching decisions

The learner starts with a precise missing-under-person observation, then locates the source account before choosing a correction. The complete main mechanism follows production response, root/list selection, attribute extraction, represented input and configured ownership comparison. That makes the reason for changing extraction visible.

Neutral keys/login/reference values replace the fictional employee without changing the evidence conditions. The response is explicitly from the compared connector production read rather than a broader administrator lookup. Correct key/login are preserved; only the wrong employeeRef input changes under review. General missing/stale causes follow the completed normal investigation rather than interrupting it.

Source documentation also describes default correlation. The supplied account is observed uncorrelated and has no other resolving path; a failed configured criterion isn't presented as a complete universal correlation algorithm. All original cases and the leading-zero/type transfer remain. Structure, diagrams and length follow the topic, without quotas or learner-facing source metadata.
