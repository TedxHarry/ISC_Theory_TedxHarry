## Explanation and full saved author read-through

Account and identity meanings establish the recipient and the record that must change. The read/export requirement gives each entitlement a useful meaning; the profile groups only these two rights from Stock-Production. Existing eligible correctly associated stock-user, allowed self-request, required review and supported hosted automatic delivery are supplied before execution.

A complete successful path connects submission, decision, supported provisioning through svc-stock, target effects, returned result and separate engineer account/use verification. Approval doesn't confer the integration actor's target privilege. UI/API contract and frontend/backend explain why the initiating interaction can end before queued asynchronous work completes.

Request, account activity, stage/status, requester/recipient and audit evidence establish what the records describe. Runtime alternatives are brief and conditional: the main SaaS path isn't assigned VA or IQService dependencies.

The partial trace keeps all earlier meaningful timestamps and exact read/export effects. A 10:08 view reflecting 09:00 collection can coexist with the 10:06 read grant. The inclusive 10:20 read reports the partial target result. Scope and the two-direction diagram explain observation versus write without making full aggregation mandatory after every write.

Question/evidence comparisons and record/time matching connect accepted decisions, operations, represented account data, current target permissions and agreed user-context checks. Aggregate Account remains scoped to the documented selected account-page action. Missing query results require the correct population, caller, filters, retained history and pages; indexing delay isn't a cure for a proven account/authority defect.

Recovery separates 11:00 prerequisite repair from the later attempt and its account/use evidence. Existing effects, running work, current authorization, conditional retry and contract-specific repeat behavior determine recovery. Hypothetical idempotence explains why repetition can have different consequences without promising all submissions or connectors are idempotent.

Timeouts preserve uncertainty until supplied target evidence settles the effect. Manual assertion and configured ticket-status completion serve tracking; they don't independently measure the intended production permission.

Revised sentences were checked in context.

All six original cases and the payroll transfer remain. Three diagrams and two tables serve this topic's flow, partial outcome, observation direction, timeline and evidence comparison; counts aren't lesson quotas.
