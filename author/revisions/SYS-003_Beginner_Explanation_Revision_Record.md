## Explanation and full read-through

The opening provides a visible wrong-manager request before defining the responsibilities needed to investigate it. Employment fact, consumed input, collected account, identity relationship and existing approval are developed in that order. Each record has a different purpose and can require a different correction.

Accountability and technical execution are connected to concrete work. The manager confirms need, the application owner explains permitted actions, the directory receives this example's membership update, and the application interprets that membership. Integration actor and recipient account are distinguished at the actual write.

Manager correlation is explained as a relationship between two identities. The field comparison identifies whose manager number and whose worker number are being compared. Account correlation is introduced separately. Both the profile's Manager Name mapping and the source's manager-correlation configuration are explicit.

The new request diagram follows a corrected relationship; it does not silently resolve or resubmit the already-pending request. That earlier assignment remains a separately inspected outcome. A failed nonempty manager match and a null input are different conditions.

Manual work remains connected to the responsible operator and actual result. General manual entitlement provisioning and certification manual remediation have different documented task routes, recorded explicitly rather than collapsed into a universal owner rule.

The author read the full saved lesson, including questions, causal answers and vendor transfer, as a simulated beginner. The review checked term introduction, pronoun/record references, transitions, successful conditions, failure alternatives and whether the next action follows from the supplied facts. It is an author simulation, not an independent reader study or proof of learner confidence. Actual beginner validation remains pending.

One text diagram and two tables were selected for the information they clarify. There is no fixed visual quota or mandatory lesson template. The historical diagrams remain available; the current diagram is saved separately as an editable [text asset](../../assets/SYS-003_manager_data_and_access_handoffs.txt).

## Example conditions and assessment review

The manager example has an effective reporting change, a correctly associated employee HR account and a selected HR-input design. M-OLD and M-NEW are illustrative identifiers. managerNumber belongs to the employee account and refers to the manager; workerNumber belongs to the intended manager identity. The successful match supplies an existing unique intended identity and supported prepared fields/configuration.

The alternative findings are alternatives, not five simultaneous defects. Finance and Customer Support belong to the separate department-mapping checkpoint. Consumed 09:10 and collected 09:15 illustrate event order, not processing latency.

The successful access path supplies an existing correctly associated directory account, a usable application account, a visible requestable self-request and the configured manager's approval. The connector supports the operation, provisioning is enabled, connectivity works and the integration has the required update authority. The directory receives the membership write. The application has recognized it when checked, and the supplied account/membership conditions satisfy the selected reading rule. Payment release remains denied. No universal activation delay or application-permission architecture is asserted.

The general manual-work variation requires a supported configured route and actual assignment. Completing a task is not independent proof of target state. Certification removal specifically uses the documented source-owner remediation route rather than importing the general entitlement-owner/fallback rule.

All four questions have enough facts for their stated next action. Answers explain why correcting a mapping differs from changing HR, delivery differs from business meaning, ownership differs from broad administration, and review differs from removal. The vendor transfer supplies read-only integration, business decision, internal assignee/vendor operator and required evidence; unresolved vendor action stays unresolved.
