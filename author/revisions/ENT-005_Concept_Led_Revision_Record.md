## Teaching decisions

Begin with the actual summary-only work requirement and ambiguous Payroll Reports label, then confirm the broader capability. Separate accepted ownership responsibility from membership before description, review and change.

Remove fictional employee/application biography, employee number, account/group keys and owner-identity stand-ins. Payroll Production retains source context. The selected employee account, payroll group and responsible payroll contact retain all necessary relationships without invented object IDs.

Complete the basic investigation and approved replacement before the deeper classification/routing/maintenance variations. The revised main example explicitly adds hypothetical outcome evidence: summary permission present, detailed-group membership absent, fresh summary use succeeds and detailed viewing/export denied. No other detailed-data grant, competing native permission change or changed work requirement is supplied. These are new teaching observations, not real tenant execution.

Introduce access request, certification/campaign, sign-off, provisioning and remediation before relying on their roles. Unsigned revoke decision versus submitted review versus delivered removal is explained in the main flow, then tested separately by Q8.

Keep administrative privilege, sensitive data, business need and native capability distinct. Direct None can mean unset classification; effective None has its own documented context. Supported inherited High is a separate example, not a claim about the payroll item's hierarchy. Manual override and automatic-method enablement are causal explanations, not UI instructions.

Two diagrams separate responsibility/holding and human evidence/decision/delivery responsibilities. Two tables compare responsible parties and field meanings. Eight original assessment purposes and changed-native-permission document transfer remain. Counts describe this lesson's needs, not a standard layout.
