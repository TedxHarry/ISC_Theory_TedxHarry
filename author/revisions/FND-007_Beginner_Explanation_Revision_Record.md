## Explanation and full author read-through

The opening gives a concrete daily-account export requirement before counting account records. The person, external accounts, collected representations, sources and connectors are explained before the one-to-many map. Deliberate administrative separation is distinguished from true duplicate accounts or duplicate representations.

Employee-number matching explains two associations with one existing identity, without merging permissions, credentials or account state. HR authority and person creation are separate from linking nonauthoritative warehouse accounts.

The successful first request supplies recipient, selected daily account, missing export/profile, requestability, completed required review and supported accepted target update. Current target and later ISC collection remain separate evidence. Wrong selected target can succeed while the business need fails, requiring correction of the extra grant as well as the missing one.

Automatic profile selection is explained through the two actual usage values before the one/zero/many outcomes. Its request and single-account exclusions remain adjacent. Conditional manual account choice is separated from the task that might perform the change.

The input-lookup mechanism is expanded using a separate concrete office example. Included stock-admin is older with office null; stock-daily is newer with office Warehouse North. Default oldest-first/non-null lookup returns Warehouse North; accountReturnFirstLink true returns the first account's null. Filter eligibility, order and value choice are different from provisioning target selection.

The author read the full saved learner in three contiguous portions, including repeat-profile, sign-in, disable/session, representation deletion and continuing-role recreation boundaries, all six worked questions and payroll transfer. Refinements expanded HR, defined credential and requestable locally, clarified profile/population wording, replaced unexplained manual fulfillment with the actual permission-change task, and defined identity refresh at the recreation explanation.

Two diagrams and five tables serve the topic; counts and length are not quotas. The new input table teaches the ordering/null distinction rather than repeating a lookup definition. Source/coverage checks and simulated author review do not establish actual independent learner comprehension. Learner validation remains pending separately from continued authorized revision.

## Example conditions and outcome controls

All people, accounts, field values and outcomes are hypothetical. No named person, company story or unnecessary object-ID stand-ins are required. Employee number 10482 is minimal comparison data only.

The existing identity has four correctly associated personal accounts across HR-Workers, Corporate-Directory and Warehouse-Production. HR-Workers supplies the authoritative population. stock-daily and stock-admin have separate approved ordinary/settings purposes; shared and nonhuman account responsibility is not modeled by correlating them to multiple humans.

Warehouse stock.read/configure/export allow viewing/settings/export in this hypothetical application. Collected usage is Daily/Admin and initial permission sets remain separate. Person-level department Warehouse Operations describes a different property from account usage.

The main first request starts with neither warehouse account holding stock.export and no Export stock reports association. Its requestable profile, permitted requester/recipient/selected daily account, completed required review and supported update are supplied. Current native evidence confirms daily export/admin unchanged; later collection is separate. No fixed execution or verification schedule is promised.

Wrong admin target, two Daily matches, one remaining admin account, existing profile, disabled admin and removed ISC row are separate changed conditions. Do not combine them into simultaneous main-path facts.

Office lookup is a separate configuration illustration. Both warehouse accounts are included, no filter applies and the transform's default created-date ascending order puts stock-admin first. Its office is null; newer stock-daily has Warehouse North. Default non-null search returns Warehouse North. True first-link mode returns null. These are conceptual outputs under supplied inputs/configuration, not a preview or execution result.

Manual selection requires organization enablement and can leave a permission-change task outstanding. Source-owner responsibility does not imply external administrative rights. The single-account exclusion means a Daily criterion alone is not a universal safety gate.

Payroll support's pay.configure/pay.export are approved and must remain. pay-review needs new export; Export payroll already belongs to the identity through the support-account request. Alternative design remains subject to requestability, account choice, authorization and support. No actual grant/configuration change is authorized by the teaching example.

The sign-in example uses current-account permissions. Actual delegated models, federation and existing-session behavior require application-specific investigation. Person employment/lifecycle state, individual account state and application session are separate facts.
