## Explanation and saved author read-through

The warning opens a question about absence before introducing any deletion setting. External account, ISC account representation and ISC identity have distinct removal targets. The main application account and HR worker record share person information but have different source keys and responsibilities.

Disabled-versus-deleted and scope/filter/pagination establish why an active-only read omits 150 of 1,000 extant records. Enable Account Deletion connects a source-level read to representation removal, with enabled/disabled, manual/scheduled and partial-file consequences.

The guard checks volume, not correctness. Main 150/1,000 = 15% exceeds supplied 10%; cancellation and app-user retention are separately supplied outcomes. Five/1,000 and equal totals with substituted identifiers expose failures that quantity alone misses.

Small-source whole-account handling is taught through the exact documented ten-account 1%/5% examples without universal edge comparator, denominator, rollback or destructive testing assumptions. Full-population, changes-only and single-account result meanings are kept separate.

Only then does the explanation vary the lost record from the application account to an authoritative HR account. Profile/mapping/priority and one-profile meaning lead to fallback/remapping or nightly deletion attempt. The outcome diagram distinguishes hidden from merely eligible and never equates eligibility with completed deletion.

Roles/access profiles, access request/work item and certification/campaign meanings support all seven blockers. Hidden identity receives its own explanation: missing profile and mapping, normal-list absence, retained account links treated as uncorrelated and shared grouping in the relevant review. Offboarding, external provisioning and retained employment-input design remain distinct.

Recovery returns to the main active-only incident: approved full coverage of 1,000, stable actor/keys/association and disabled external account unchanged. Prior removal or authoritative/hidden effects need state-specific supported recovery and cannot promise original ISC IDs/history/relationships.
