## Teaching decisions

The main records and explicit policies establish a complete approved reference comparison before its reliability is examined through uniqueness scope, availability, representation and stability. Neutral sources/accounts replace biography and pseudo identity ID without removing person-versus-employee reference meaning or eligible/unique/explicit-link/default conditions.

The two relationship diagrams explain legitimate multiple accounts and temporal email reuse rather than private execution. Current uniqueness isn't overclaimed as history. Reference persistence is a supplied organizational policy, not a universal HR/ISC guarantee.

Metrics remain deliberately separate: 1,000 equal emails/one confirmed wrong owner and 970 verified reference values/30 missing. The lesson does not infer all remaining email ownership, 30 uncorrelated results or blanket rollout approval. Normalization preserves namespace distinctions, and separate standard criteria aren't treated as implicit composite AND.

All original purposes, eight cases and acquisition transfer remain. Teaching structure and counts follow this topic rather than quotas.
