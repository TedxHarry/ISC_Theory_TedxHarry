## Changes and teaching decisions

The lesson establishes the collected business input, assigned lifecycle, participation setting and actual account status through configuration and normal leave evidence. Existing sync eligibility/delivery is explained before the operation comparison. Recurring conditions, full exit/partial delivery, later manual correction and defaults/reconciliation follow with their own evidence.

Fictional worker/employee/account/object IDs and source brands are removed. HR-Primary/Directory-Primary preserve input/target boundaries. EMP/LEAVE/EXIT and technical employed/leave/retained are approved example contracts. No label-basedstate, day-count progression or target-disable guarantee.

The first table replaces the old four-meaning table with actual three-stage mapping/configuration; all four information distinctions remain explicit in prose and the two-branch diagram. The second table preserves five operational comparison purposes. Two diagrams serve configurationbranches and supplied observations; no quota imposed.

The short-term case completes with approvedenabledaccount/LEAVEfield and knownrecipientexclusion. The long-term case completes currentstate/finalfieldsync/faileddisable/freshenabled checks. LaterEXIT-CORRpromotion/manualsync is separate; fieldrecovery doesn't establishcurrentdisablement. No specific latecorrectiontime added to learner.

Full officialHTMLstate matrix was parsed/read as rows, with ordinary longtermsyncexclusion qualified by lifecycle's final/manual exceptions. No statebehavior of nextProcessing inferred from recurringrules. Preview and periodicrefresh receive meanings before reliance.

Eight prompts precede separate worked reasoning; contractor transfer checks appliedemail before manualtargetalignment without makingcontractactive.
