## Explanation and full author read-through

The opening starts with required work on accounts. Entitlement meaning follows its source/permission context. The invoice/supplier relationship explains why a profile is useful before introducing the role that combines the wider responsibility.

A containment map distinguishes included objects from an execution sequence. Direct role entitlements are explicitly supported; a one-item profile is a useful design choice rather than a mandatory layer. Shared references, ownership and native application roles are explained through their different jobs.

Collection and person association are introduced when the recipient and destination matter. Each automatic comparison is decoded before AND is used as a decision. Configuration readiness, successful evaluation and actual account delivery have separate facts and evidence.

The second diagram follows the matching identity to assignment and required changes on two accounts. Both successful target outcomes are supplied. Branches do not promise atomic execution, timing or rollback. Person qualification and account selection are separate decisions.

The author read the complete saved learner in three contiguous portions, including all six worked checks, removal cases and support transfer, as a simulated beginner. The read-through clarified a directory group, replaced unexplained population wording with group-of-people configuration, moved connector meaning to first operational use, explained the service purpose before naming Provisioning and aligned the two receiving-account columns.

The partial-result explanation now states the memberships/permissions actually delivered without claiming complete application use. Existing-access detection explicitly recognizes an already-defined profile rather than creating a new definition and keeps original grant history separate. Assignment removal, overlap, definition deletion, content-removal propagation and account deletion remain different operations. The added FinanceWorkspace content-edit variation explains disabled versus enabled propagation while the role remains assigned, retaining target-result and remaining-requirement checks.

## Example conditions and outcome controls

The employee and source permissions, profiles, role labels and outcomes are hypothetical. No company story, named person, invented employee number or username is needed. Main source names are Invoice-System and Corporate-Directory. InvoiceRead and SupplierRead are finance items; FinanceWorkspace is a directory item.

The main identity and both accounts already exist with correct correlation and one relevant account per source. The connector arrangements support reading and the illustrated membership changes. The role and profile are enabled, assignment settings prepared and required Provisioning service available.

The identity currently holds department Finance and jobCode AP_READER. Both comparisons match. AP_MANAGER fails the second condition; title similarity does not participate. Real eligibility must include all required business conditions.

No listed access is preexisting on the main path, no competing assignment/origin exists and no separate activation is required. Both target acceptances are supplied, not inferred from a role-assignment screen. No simultaneous order, atomic cross-system transaction, automatic rollback or immediate full collection is promised.

Partial failure changes only the outcome: FinanceWorkspace and InvoiceRead are accepted, SupplierRead rejected for insufficient integration authority. Delivered permissions do not by themselves prove every application-use requirement. Recovery needs authorized correction, current/outstanding work, supported reattempt and actual missing-item verification.

Detection changes the starting state: both finance permissions already exist with no new grant supplied. Collection and processing explain the represented profile, without proving original delivery or approval. It neither creates the directory membership nor establishes the wider role.

Removal first supplies sole assignment through the old role. The overlap variation introduces another assigned role requiring InvoiceRead. It does not infer identical retention behavior across every origin. Object disablement/deletion is not revocation. Role-content removal propagation is a separate configured mechanism, and entitlement removal is not account deletion.

Support transfer uses View tickets/Add internal notes in one ticketing source and Knowledge reader in a separate knowledge-base source. Existing View tickets is not new-grant evidence; the rejected knowledge operation leaves that requirement unfulfilled even if the other ticketing change succeeds.

No tenant operation, grant, role/profile change, recovery, revocation, account deletion, lab result or learner study was performed. Full access profiles, role assignment/overlap/propagation and provisioning details remain in their primary lessons; this learner supplies the required local meanings and logical connections.
