## Explanation and complete author read-through

The opening identifies the service whose password is forgotten. Account and identity meanings establish why working ISC sign-in doesn't determine an independent application's password. The record table separates responsibilities without claiming every directory/IdP row must have its own credential store.

Authentication, authorization and sessions explain sign-in versus page access versus permitted business work. SSO, IdP, service provider, federation, SAML and assertion lead into a complete successful browser path with existing matching identity and JIT off. Trust acceptance and intended-user matching then explain distinct failure boundaries; signing information isn't presented as a substitute for all SAML acceptance checks.

The email comparison supplies exact successful and mismatched values, an explicit matching error and the intended tenant. Separate JIT source/profile/mappings establish optional account/identity creation rather than default SSO behavior or all-app creation.

The separate PTA setup supplies supported configured authentication source, prior aggregation, correct association and one directory-user account. Normal SSO precedence remains explicit. The VA is conditional on the actual integration, and first-encountered PTA account selection isn't replaced with display order or provisioning selection.

MFA factor categories and TOTP establish registration versus challenge. Identity-profile requirements, elevated initial registration, later normal IdP sign-ins and direct-route requirements are distinct. Correct-service recovery and dedicated MFA reset remain separate from identity deregistration.

The standalone password trace supplies Support-Production, visible access application, eligible support-user, independent authentication, usable recovery, compatible policies and svc-support target authority. It completes the successful target change and returned result before engineer verification. User recovery verification doesn't grant connector authority; connection/read success doesn't prove password-write capability.

Shared-source passwords and multi-source sync groups retain different mechanisms. The PTA failure gate and non-PTA partial result remain conditional; no rollback, reverse synchronization or readable history is invented. Recovery email, disabled/locked account states, fresh authentication, independent sessions, browser retention, unsupported SAML SLO and prepared emergency access retain their original explanatory purposes.

Refinements expanded HR, clarified IdP proof-checking, digital signatures and session cookies, split elevated-user conditions for readability and added explicit identity-profile MFA configuration. The final refinements were reviewed against the saved content.

All seven original worked cases and the purchasing proposal transfer remain. Three diagrams and two tables serve the three operations and two comparisons; their counts follow this topic.
