## Teaching decisions

Start with confirmed selected removal and continued reading, then establish membership versus permission. Explain the direct and nested relationships, their direction and the application's complete rule before returning to the removal. Main-case acceptance precedes policy variations, representation and broader investigation.

Remove Devi's biography/employee number, fictional application brand and arbitrary account/group IDs. Reports Production retains the source context. Native support-team/report-readers/exception-readers values preserve exact relationship interpretation without invented ISC IDs. The selected account is correctly associated with its owner; the account used remains attached to the permission question.

The application policy supplies two alternative reading routes, with enabled-account, successful-sign-in, West-resource and read-action conditions. Exception Readers is independent, not a regional bypass. Each result is a fresh permission decision against the supplied current relationships, not an old-session observation. Main removal leaves other relationships, policy and account conditions unchanged.

The same-kind nested relationship and schema interpretation are supplied supported integration facts. parentGroups explicitly means containing groups. hierarchyAttribute names the field supplying hierarchy references; the example's interpretation is not a universal connector field, parent/child convention, payload or configuration recipe.

Two diagrams support the two routes and representation-versus-authorization boundary. Two tables explain rules and six changed-condition outcomes. Eight original assessment purposes and the native application-role transfer remain. Counts describe this lesson, not a required template.

The transfer's final alternative is explicitly a separate variation with its full account membership and direct-only policy supplied. It no longer calls multiple changed facts “one fact,” nor leaves an earlier removal's after-state to be inferred.
