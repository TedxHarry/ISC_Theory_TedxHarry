## Example conditions and worked answers

Read reports and Export reports are illustrative native permissions represented as entitlements. The main account exists, is enabled, is correctly associated with the intended identity and is the signed-in account used for checks. The daily operations report collection is in scope. Its selected rule checks current permissions per operation, with no alternative export grant.

The 08:45 collection obtained both permissions. The 09:00 deadline is a business requirement. The 09:02 operation is rejected at 09:03 because of missing integration removal privilege. Current target evidence at 09:05 and a safe successful export at 09:06 support the finding that restriction is unmet.

The separate authorized manual correction occurs at 09:12 after establishing no continuing configured restoration requirement. Reading/enabled status remain. Target observation at 09:13 and application checks at 09:14 verify retained reading and permission-based export denial. ISC's 09:15 view is still the 08:45 observation; the permitted 09:20 collection incorporates the correction. No fixed latency, automatic round trip or retrospective success of the rejected operation is implied.

09:30 inspection and the 10:30/11:00/11:10 checkpoint distinguish source observation from inspection time. All times are hypothetical and comparable in one day/zone. Search time-zone behavior is taught separately.

The group variant explicitly changes the native rule to direct export OR group membership; removal makes only the direct condition false. The session variant explicitly changes current checking to earlier permission information. The continuing requested-entitlement case is a separate assignment condition, not imported into the manual-correction timeline.

All four worked answers preserve uncertainty where evidence is missing. The empty Search case does not justify adding access. The missing direct grant does not identify the specific alternative cause of continued export.

The portal transfer uses standard-account and admin-account only to distinguish two required records. Unknown admin-account state, unknown collection time and service unavailability leave the person-wide restriction unverified. Safe authorization-specific evidence must cover both accounts, relevant grants/sessions and retained reading; no real bank data change is requested. Coordinated parallel or other appropriate check orders are accepted.
