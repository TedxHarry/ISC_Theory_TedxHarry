- One existing identity is correctly associated with four personal account records across three sources. The two warehouse accounts are on the same production source and have separately approved purposes.
- HR-Workers is the supplied authoritative population. Warehouse account employeeNumber and the existing identity's matching attribute have 10482, uniquely and correctly identifying this person in the example. Copied/reused values require investigation.
- Daily/admin usage values and stock.read/configure/export meanings belong to the hypothetical warehouse application. They are not SailPoint defaults. No service account is correlated to several humans.
- Main request path starts with neither warehouse account holding export and the identity not already holding the profile. It assumes requestability, permitted requester/recipient/account choice, required review, supported update and supplied successful target evidence.
- Already-held profile, wrong target, zero/multiple matches, one remaining account and disabled/removed accounts are separate variations, not simultaneous facts in the normal request.
- Automatic criteria apply only in their documented scope. Manual account selection requires organization enablement; account choice and possible manual fulfillment remain different work.
