## Assessment and evaluation

The worked questions are learning material, not results of a learner test.

- Missing account: tests design limitation versus collection/association problem; acceptable reasoning first verifies absence and follows the approved exception or designs an authorized extension.
- One recovery: tests impact scope and residual failed work, especially removals.
- Reviewer reversal: tests authority, current execution state and separately authorized restoration.
- Deleted package: tests configuration versus delivered permissions and compensating correction.
- Unfamiliar service: tests the complete requirement/capability/acceptance/handover/recovery loop without a familiar character or application.
- Implicit teach-back: asks whether the learner can connect the four responsibilities, distinguish decisions from delivery, and identify evidence rather than quote definitions alone.

When comparing the pilot with the original, ask readers to explain the responsibilities in their own words and reason through unseen evidence before revealing worked answers. Compare reading effort, concept accuracy, dependency tracing and safe next checks. Preference alone does not establish improved understanding; original-first reading can also influence a comparison.
